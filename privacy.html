Privacy Policy

Wadhwa Solar ("we", "us") installs and maintains rooftop solar systems in Panipat, Karnal, Sonipat and Gurugram, Haryana. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

If anything here is unclear, write to hello@wadhwasolar.com or call +91 70277 63605.

What we collect

When you contact us on WhatsApp

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name
  • The messages you send us, and the options you select in our automated chat
  • Your average monthly electricity bill amount, as you tell us
  • Your city or locality, and your property address once you book a site survey
  • Your answers about roof ownership, bill name and available roof area

When you use this website

  • Details you enter into the enquiry form: name, phone number, city, property type and bill amount
  • Standard server logs kept by our hosting provider

When you become a customer

  • Installation address, electricity connection number and system specification
  • Documents required for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy application, which are submitted to the government portal and your electricity distribution company
  • Service and maintenance history for your system

We do not collect your Aadhaar number, bank details or identity documents through WhatsApp or this website. Those are handled directly on the official government portal or shared with us in person during the subsidy application.

Why we use it

  • To estimate a system size and cost from your electricity bill
  • To arrange, confirm and remind you about a free site survey
  • To prepare a written quotation
  • To file and follow up your subsidy and net-metering applications
  • To schedule cleaning, maintenance and service visits
  • To send you updates about your installation, and, if you have opted in, occasional offers and scheme updates

WhatsApp messaging and consent

We use the WhatsApp Business Platform to talk to customers and enquiries.

  • We reply to you when you message us first
  • We send service messages related to your enquiry, survey or installation
  • We send promotional messages only if you have opted in, for example by clicking an advertisement that opens a WhatsApp chat, or by ticking a consent box
  • You can stop promotional messages at any time by replying STOP. We will still send messages necessary for an installation already in progress

Message content is processed by WhatsApp under its own terms, and stored by us as described below.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

We share it only where necessary:

  • Government portals and agencies — pmsuryaghar.gov.in, MNRE and HAREDA, for your subsidy application
  • Your electricity distribution company — UHBVN or DHBVN, for feasibility approval, net metering and inspection
  • Service providers we use to run the business — WhatsApp Business Platform for messaging, and hosting and database providers that store our records securely
  • Where the law requires it — in response to a lawful request from a government authority

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become customers: up to 24 months, then deleted
  • Customer records: for the life of the system warranty and any period required by tax and subsidy rules
  • WhatsApp message history: up to 24 months

How we protect it

Records are stored in access-controlled systems. Only people who need the information to do their job can see it. We use encrypted connections for data in transit and do not store payment card details at any point.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong or out of date
  • Delete your information, where we are not required to keep it
  • Withdraw your consent to marketing messages
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf

Write to hello@wadhwasolar.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

Our services are meant for property owners and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be notified to customers directly.
