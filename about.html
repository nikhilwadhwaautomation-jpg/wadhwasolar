What a good rooftop actually earns

Asked before every install

How long is my roof occupied?

Two to four days on site for a home. Panels sit on a raised structure, so most of the terrace stays usable underneath.

Will it work in a power cut?

A standard on-grid system shuts off during a cut, for lineman safety. If you need backup, we fit a hybrid inverter with a battery.

How often does it need cleaning?

Every two to four weeks in dusty months, less in monsoon. Our plans cover it, or we will show you how to do it safely yourself.

What warranties come with it?

Typically 25 years performance and 10 to 12 years product on panels, 5 to 10 years on the inverter, plus our own workmanship warranty on structure and wiring.
