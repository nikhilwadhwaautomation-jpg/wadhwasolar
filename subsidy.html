Eligibility

Who can apply

Any income level

No income limit, no urban or rural restriction. You need a grid connection in your own name.

Your own roof

Owners apply directly. Tenants need a no-objection letter, since the subsidy is paid to the owner.

Enough clear roof

About 100 sq ft per kW, shadow-free between 9am and 4pm. We confirm this at the survey.

Documents

Four papers, that is all

01

Latest electricity bill

In the applicant's name, from UHBVN or DHBVN. Six months of bills help us size the system.

02

Aadhaar card

Linked to the mobile number you will use on the portal.

03

Bank passbook or cancelled cheque

Aadhaar-seeded account, because the subsidy is a direct transfer.

04

Ownership proof or NOC

Registry, allotment letter, or a signed no-objection letter from the owner.

Common questions

When does the money arrive?

Usually 30 to 45 days after the DISCOM inspects the system and you raise the redemption request. It is credited to your bank account, not adjusted against our bill.

Do I pay full price first?

Yes. You pay for the system and the subsidy comes to you afterwards. We can also connect you to rooftop solar loans if you want to spread the cost.

Why do applications get rejected?

Usually the panels or inverter are not on the MNRE ALMM list, the bill is in another family member's name, or the bank account is not Aadhaar-seeded. We check all three before you order anything.

What happens to extra units?

They go to the grid through a net meter and are credited against your consumption. Credits carry forward, so summer surplus covers winter use.

Is there a Haryana state top-up?

State schemes change year to year. We check what is live when you apply and tell you plainly if there is nothing extra.
