Data Deletion Instructions

You can ask Wadhwa Solar to delete the personal information we hold about you at any time. This page explains how.

How to request deletion

  1. Email hello@wadhwasolar.com with the subject line Delete my data, or send us a WhatsApp message on +91 70277 63605.
  2. Tell us the phone number or email address you used when you contacted us, so we can find your records.
  3. We will confirm we received your request within 3 working days.
  4. Your data will be deleted within 30 days, and we will confirm once it is done.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, phone number and city
  • Your WhatsApp conversation history with us
  • Electricity bill amounts and the quotation figures we generated for you
  • Site survey notes and appointment records
  • Your marketing consent record, after which you will receive no further messages

What we may have to keep

Some records cannot be deleted immediately because the law requires us to retain them:

  • Completed installations — invoices and tax records must be kept for the period required under Indian tax law
  • Subsidy applications — documents already submitted to the government portal or your electricity distribution company are held by those bodies under their own rules, and we cannot delete them on your behalf
  • Warranty records — retained for the life of your system warranty, unless you tell us you accept losing warranty support

We will tell you exactly what is being retained and why when we confirm your request.

Stopping messages without deleting your data

If you only want to stop promotional messages, reply STOP to any WhatsApp message from us. Your enquiry record stays, but you will not receive offers or scheme updates.
