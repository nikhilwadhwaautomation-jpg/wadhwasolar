How it goes

Roof to running, in four to six weeks

1. Free survey

We measure the shadow-free area, read your bill and check the roof structure.

2. Written quote

Size, brands, price, subsidy and expected units. On paper, not on a phone call.

3. Approvals

Portal registration and UHBVN / DHBVN feasibility approval, filed by us.

4. Installation

Structure, modules, inverter, cabling and earthing. Two to four days for a home.

5. Net meter

The DISCOM fits the bidirectional meter and inspects. Your meter starts running backwards.

6. Subsidy and care

Subsidy lands in your bank account. We stay on for cleaning and service.
